### Why this wasn't finished automatically
Both apps were **created by the `admin@recruiterstack.in` Slack account**, so the
"Delete App" button lives in the **admin@ developer console**. The browser session used
during the build was stuck on `tech@`, and Slack's dev console offers **no
account-switcher** (only "add another workspace") — switching would require a full
sign-out/re-login as admin@, which wasn't worth the session-tangle risk just for cleanup.

### How to finish it (~1 min each)
From the **admin@recruiterstack.in** Google account in Chrome:
1. Go to **https://api.slack.com/apps**
2. **Hiring_Updates** → *Basic Information* → scroll to bottom → **Delete App** → confirm
3. Same for **Org_Level**

(Alternatively, a workspace Owner can remove them via
`app.slack.com/apps-manage/T0AJCLZKCTE/integrations/installed`, but the remove control
was finicky for these custom apps.)
